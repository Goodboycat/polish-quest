Short and plain. Last updated 2026-11-11.

THE SHORT VERSION

Your game zip is opened inside your own browser. It is not uploaded. This page has no accounts, no tracking, no ads and loads nothing from other websites.

WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR ZIP

  • You drop the zip. Your browser reads the list of files and some of the text files, on your device.
  • The page works out your engine, type of game and what the game already has. It shows you the result.
  • The zip and the code stay in your browser memory. When you close the tab they are gone.
  • No part of the zip is sent anywhere. There is no upload code on this page.

WHAT IS SAVED ON YOUR DEVICE

  • A short summary in your browser storage (localStorage): game name, engine, type, your weakest phone, the list of protected files, which prompts you ticked off, and what you marked as already in the game.
  • It is saved so you do not start over when you come back. It stays on this device only.
  • It does not contain your code or your zip.
  • The "Forget" button on the page deletes it. You can also clear your browser site data.

WHAT THIS PAGE DOES NOT DO

  • No accounts or logins.
  • No cookies set by this page.
  • No analytics, trackers, ads or fingerprinting.
  • No fonts, scripts, pictures or anything else loaded from other websites. The font and the art are files on this site.
  • No AI service is called. The prompts are built by plain code on this page, from your tap choices.

HOW THE BROWSER ENFORCES IT

The page includes a Content Security Policy. It tells your browser to refuse any network connection from the page (connect-src none) and to load files only from this page itself. This is a rule your browser applies, so even a mistake in the page code cannot send data out through normal web requests.

You can check it yourself: use airplane mode (see the "Is it safe?" screen), or open your browser developer tools and watch the Network tab while you drop a zip. You should see no request go out.

OTHER COMPANIES

Whoever hosts this page can see, like any website host, that the page was opened (for example an address and a time). They cannot see your zip, because it never leaves your device. The host's name is shown on the "Is it safe?" screen when the owner has filled it in.

If a payment is ever involved, the company that takes the payment is named right where you pay. This page itself takes no payments.

Links you tap that go to other websites (like the contact link for a custom review) take you to those websites, which have their own rules.

THE CUSTOM REVIEW

The review screen builds a short text summary on your device. You can read every word of it. Nothing is sent from the page. If you want a review, you copy the summary and send it yourself, to the person named on that screen. You choose whether to send it at all.

WHAT I CANNOT PROMISE

I cannot control your browser, your browser extensions, or your device. These checks are about this page. If you use a shared computer, use the Forget button when you finish.
