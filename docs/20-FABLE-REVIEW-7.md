# Fable 5.2 screenshot review (2026-11-14, shots: live10)

SCORE: 7.5
PROFESSIONAL: 7  BEGINNER: 8  ALIVE: 6  MONEY-READY: 6

TOP FIVE DEFECTS:
1. desktop-2-kind (question screen): the three answer cards are tall empty boxes with two lines of text at the top, and the selected 2D card sits 4px higher so the row looks misaligned. Put a simple picture in each card (a 3D cube, a 2D sprite, a card or menu) to fill the dead space, or cut the card height to about 120px. Keep all cards on the same baseline and show selection with the gold border plus a tick only. Add a gold "Next" button; right now only "Back" and "Skip this" are visible, so a beginner cannot tell how to go forward.
2. desktop-6-plan: the checkout flow, "Pay, then email me your game", reads like a hobby side project and kills trust at the moment of payment. Replace it with an upload or link field shown right after payment. State a delivery time and a refund line next to the Order button. Rename the grey "Invented example task" bar to "Sample task" and style it as a small gold label rather than a disabled-looking grey bar. Move the Back button so it is not hanging at the fold.
3. desktop-5-assets: the Mixamo row is sliced in half by the search bar, which looks like a rendering bug. The licence, the most important fact on this screen, is plain grey text. Show exactly three full rows per page with a "More" control, or give the list its own clearly scrolling area with a fade. Turn the licence into a pill badge: green "CC0" and amber "Free account, not open source". Move the Poly Haven search directly under the Poly Haven row, or onto its own tab.
4. desktop-1-home and phone-1-home: the hero polyhedron is dark and near invisible on home, kind, overview and assets, while it is bright gold on the rating screen, so most of the site still looks dead. On the phone it is a murky smudge that takes up the top 250px and pushes the headline down. Use the lit gold version everywhere with slow rotation. On the phone, shrink it to about 120px or drop it. Also on the phone, the "Start here" hand overlaps the edge of the button; move it 12px above.
5. desktop-3-rating and phone-3-rating: the status labels are inconsistent. "Weak" has a diamond and "Needs work" has none, and both are the same white. Make "Weak" one accent colour with the diamond and "Needs work" a muted grey. The "Free, with ads or purchases" and "Change" row is cramped and wraps awkwardly beside the ring; put it on one line under the title as a small chip. On the phone, "Report and AI opinion" is gold while "See the plan" is white. Make both the same quiet white link, on one row.

NEXT PROMPT FOR THE BUILDER: Fix the question screens first, because every user passes through them. On the PC/Mobile, game type and look screens, give each answer card a simple gold line illustration that fills the empty space. Keep all cards the same height and on the same baseline. Show the selected card with a gold border and a tick, with no vertical shift. Add a clear gold "Next" button to the right of "Back", disabled until a card is picked, and keep "Skip this" as a quiet link. Then use the bright gold, slowly rotating polyhedron from the rating screen on every screen, at about 120px on the phone, so the site no longer looks dead outside the rating page.
