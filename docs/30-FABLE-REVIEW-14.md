# Fable 5.2 screenshot review (2026-11-14, shots: live18)

SCORE: 6.5
PROFESSIONAL: 6.5  BEGINNER: 7  ALIVE: 7  MONEY-READY: 7

TOP FIVE DEFECTS:
1. phone-home: The page looks disabled. The headline, body text and both buttons are washed out as if a dim overlay or unfinished fade is stuck on. The hand still overlaps the top edge of the Choose button, and there is still a dead gap of about 200px between the privacy line and the buttons. Fix: make all content full opacity on the first frame. Put the two buttons directly under the privacy line, in the flow, not in a fixed bottom bar. Put the hand and "Start here" label below or beside the button with at least 8px clearance and no overlap.
2. desktop-home: The hand sits under the gap between the two buttons, and the "Start here" label sits under "Try the example game". The hand looks greyed out and the label is a muddy dim gold, so the cue is ambiguous and looks broken. Fix: anchor the hand's fingertip to the horizontal centre of the Choose button, 8px below it. Put the label to the hand's left or directly under the Choose button. Render both at full opacity from frame one.
3. desktop-assets: The primary button says "Copy prompt" with no hint of what prompt it copies. The "Coin Hop asset list" link is still orphaned between the list and the buttons. The gold underlined provider names compete with the gold tab, gold "Show more", gold card border and two gold buttons. Fix: rename the button to say what it copies, e.g. "Copy asset prompt", and add a one-line caption. Move the "Coin Hop asset list" link into the Coin Hop card next to Play. Make the provider names white with a small external-link icon.
4. desktop-plan / phone-plan-menu: The paid card is crowded and hurts trust. The fine print is jammed against the card's bottom edge. "No results are guaranteed" sits directly under the refund line. Step 2 is an underlined link while steps 1 and 3 are plain text. The sample panel is labelled "(invented)". On the phone, a fixed full-width Back button takes the prime bottom slot while "Order for $25" scrolls away. Fix: add 16px of padding under the fine print and soften it to "You run the prompts yourself." Make all three steps plain text. Relabel the sample as "Example task". On the phone, pin "Order for $25" at the bottom and demote Back to a small text link at the top.
5. desktop-kind / desktop-rating: The right-hand gem is a different size, position and lighting on every screen, and on the rating screen it is washed out to pale yellow, so it reads as a glitch rather than as life. On the kind screen the hand points at the gap between the cards, and the disabled dashed "Next" looks broken. Fix: lock the gem to one size and one anchor on all inner screens and limit the brightness so it never blows out. Centre the hand under the PC card. Make the disabled "Next" a solid button at low contrast with no dashed border.

NEXT PROMPT FOR THE BUILDER: Fix phone home first. All text and both buttons must be full opacity on the first painted frame, with no dim layer and no fade that can stall. Move the two buttons out of the fixed bottom bar to sit directly under the privacy line so the dead gap is gone. Place the hand and "Start here" below the Choose button with 8px clearance, never overlapping it. On desktop home and on the kind screen, anchor the hand's fingertip to the centre of its target (the Choose button, the PC card) with the label on the side away from any other button, both at full opacity. On assets, rename "Copy prompt" to say what it copies, move the "Coin Hop asset list" link into the Coin Hop card, and make the provider names white instead of gold. On the plan screen, add bottom padding under the fine print, remove "No results are guaranteed" from beside the refund promise, make the three steps consistent plain text, relabel the sample as "Example task", and on phone pin "Order for $25" at the bottom instead of Back. Lock the gem to one size, position and brightness limit across all inner screens. Change nothing else.
