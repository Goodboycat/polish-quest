# 2. First client: plan, message, price, delivery

## Honest note on Genspark
I found no public free Genspark API. So: do the review **by hand, one time**. No key and no AI call ever goes inside the website.

## How the by-hand review works
1. The client uses the site on their own device. In the **Review** screen, the site builds a short summary (no code, no zip). The client reads it before sending.
2. The client sends you only that summary (text). You paste it to Genspark once and ask for a polished roadmap.
3. You check the roadmap yourself, fix anything that looks invented, then send it back.

## Tell the client, in plain words, before they send anything
"You will send me one short text summary. It has: the engine, game type, which features are already there, your weakest phone, and the one thing that hurts most. It has NO code, NO zip and NO files. You can read it first. I will paste it into an AI tool (Genspark) once to help write your roadmap. Nobody else gets it. I will delete it after I send your roadmap."
Only promise the deletion if you will really do it.

## Where to find client #1 for free
- Your own itch.io followers and devlog comments.
- People who already asked you for help with their game.
- itch.io game jam pages: look for devs who say "stuck", "buggy" or "needs polish" in comments. Reply with help first, not with a pitch.
- Read each community's rules before posting. r/gamedev bans self-promotion. r/indiegames bans AI-made games. Re-check live rules.

## Message to copy
"Hi [name]! I saw [their game] and loved [one real thing]. I made a free tool that reads your game zip on YOUR device (nothing is uploaded) and gives you a numbered list of prompts for your AI coding tool, in the right order, so fixes do not break what works. I am looking for one developer to try a custom review: I turn your summary into a short roadmap for your game. Test price: $19. If it does not help, tell me and I will refund you. Want to try?"
Replace the bracketed words yourself before sending.

## Price
$19 as a test is fair for ONE person's first review. It is low enough to say yes to, and high enough that they will read it. After 3 happy clients, move to $29 or $39. Do not offer a refund you cannot pay.

## Make him happy (deliver this)
1. A roadmap of 8-12 steps, ordered, each saying why now.
2. The top 3 fixes marked "do this today".
3. Which of his files NOT to touch.
4. A "what to test after each step" line.
5. A reply within 48 hours (say the time in advance).
6. Ask for a short quote he is happy for you to use. Do not ask for a review before he has results.
