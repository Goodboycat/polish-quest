# Fable 5.2 screenshot review (2026-11-14, shots: live17)

SCORE: 6.5
PROFESSIONAL: 6  BEGINNER: 7  ALIVE: 7.5  MONEY-READY: 7

TOP FIVE DEFECTS:
1. phone-home: The hand and "Start here" label still sit on top of the "Try the example game" button and cover its text. This was the headline defect last time and it is not fixed on phone. Fix: anchor the label above the Choose button's top edge, pointing down, and never let it overlap any button. If it cannot fit above the sticky bar, hide the hand on phone and keep only the ring.
2. desktop-home: "Start here" floats at the bottom edge, about 150px below and to the right of the Choose button, with the hand cut off by the fold. It points at nothing. Fix: place the hand and label directly under the Choose button, within about 12px of it and fully inside the viewport, or remove them and rely on the gold ring.
3. phone-home: There is a dead black gap of about 220px between "Your zip never leaves your device" and the sticky buttons, so the screen looks empty and unfinished. Fix: vertically centre the hero block in the space above the sticky bar, or make the gem larger to fill the gap.
4. desktop-assets: Gold is still overused, and the orphan link is still there. Gold appears on the three CC0 pills, the link names, the active tab, "Show more", the card border, Play and Copy prompt, and "Coin Hop asset list" sits alone between the list and the buttons. Fix: make the CC0 pills a neutral grey outline, make link names white with an underline, move "Coin Hop asset list" inside the Coin Hop card under Play, and make "Copy prompt" the only filled gold button, with Play as an outline.
5. desktop-plan: The left card is cramped and the right sample card is plain. The three pay steps are uneven columns, with "The plan comes by email" wrapping to three lines, and the PayPal disclaimer is jammed against the card's bottom edge. The big gem that appears on the other desktop screens is missing here, so the page feels flatter. The open phone menu also lies over the plan page with no dimming, which looks messy. Fix: use three equal-width step columns, add 16px of padding above the disclaimer, and put a dim backdrop behind the open phone menu.

NEXT PROMPT FOR THE BUILDER: Fix the home pointer properly on both sizes. The hand and "Start here" label must sit right next to the "Choose your game .zip" button, fully on screen, and must never overlap "Try the example game". On phone, put the label above the sticky bar pointing down at the Choose button, or hide the hand and keep only the ring. On phone home, centre the hero text in the free space so there is no big empty gap. On the assets screen, turn the CC0 pills and link names neutral, move "Coin Hop asset list" into the Coin Hop card, and leave only one filled gold button. On the plan screen, make the three pay steps equal width, add padding above the PayPal line, and add a dim backdrop behind the open phone menu. Do not add any new text.
