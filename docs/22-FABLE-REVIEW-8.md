Old complaints: "looked dead" and "too many colors" are fixed. "Too much text" is mostly fixed. "One screen at a time" still fails on the assets and plan screens. "Not professional" still fails on phone, where there are visible clipping bugs.

TOP FIVE DEFECTS:
1. **phone-home:** The primary button text is clipped to "ue Moon Cat Runner," and there is a dead black gap of about 150px between the shape and the headline. Fix: shorten the label to "Continue" with the game name on a small line above it, let the button text wrap or shrink and never overflow, and pull the headline up directly under the shape.
2. **desktop-plan:** The sample task card sits on top of the gold shape, which pokes out from behind it and looks like a layering bug. The screen also still states no refund, delivery time or author, so a buyer has no reason to trust the $25. Fix: hide or shrink the shape on this screen, or put the card in its own column clear of it. Reserve one line under the Order button for delivery time and refund, to be filled the moment the owner decides. Money-ready cannot pass 6 until then.
3. **desktop-assets:** The list is cut off mid-row at "Mesh2Motion", the search bar overlaps the list, and the screen carries tabs, a list, a dropdown, a search, a Coin Hop link, an asset list link and two buttons all at once. The playable example is the best proof on the site and it is buried as a small text link. Fix: show three full rows per tab with a "Show more" control. Turn Coin Hop into a visible card with a thumbnail and a Play button. Move the search to its own tab.
4. **desktop-rating and phone-rating:** The goal chip truncates to "Free, with ads or purc…" on desktop and "Free, with ads …" on phone. "Weak◆" has a diamond glued to the word. The disclaimer "This is the made-up example game" is tiny grey text. Fix: use a short chip label such as "Free game" or let it wrap. Put a space before the diamond or remove it. Show "Example game" as a clear badge beside the title.
5. **desktop-kind:** The hand pointer floats in empty space to the right of "Skip this", pointing at neither card, and stray sparkles drift down toward the bottom of the page. The PC icon is a terminal prompt, which reads as "coding" rather than "play on a computer". The disabled Next button looks almost the same as an active one. Fix: anchor the hand on the first card and keep the sparkles within about 40px of it. Use a plain monitor or gamepad icon. Dim Next to an obviously disabled state.

Close the dead gap on phone home. Stop the assets list cutting off mid-row behind the search bar by showing three full rows with "Show more". Keep the gold shape from ever sitting behind or touching a card, and hide it on the plan screen. Anchor the hand pointer directly on the first choice card with its sparkles contained near it. Then promote Coin Hop on the assets screen from a text link to a card with a thumbnail and a Play button.
