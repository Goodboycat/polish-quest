# 4. Marketing: first 30 days, only free
Do one small thing a day. Never post anything the tool did not really do. Never say "tested on" a model you did not test.

## Rules first
Read the rules of each place before you post. Use the sidebar and the pinned post. What I verified on 2026-11-11: itch.io forbids ads on other projects' pages and boards and wants self-promotion on the Release announcements board. r/gamedev's posting guidelines page lets you share a tool for developers, and puts game promotion in weekly threads (Feedback Friday, Screenshot Saturday, Marketing Monday); I could not read its live sidebar. r/indiegames: not verified. When unsure, ask a moderator first or post only help.

## Week 1: build proof
- Day 1: Put the site live (see 01). Test airplane mode.
- Day 2: Run the tool on 5 free real games (open-source demos). Screenshot the scan screen.
- Day 3: Make a before/after clip (15 seconds): messy prompt list vs numbered prompts.
- Day 4: Post a devlog on your itch.io page: "I made a tool that reads your zip on your device".
- Day 5: Add the "test it yourself" steps to the post.
- Day 6: Reply to 5 dev questions with real help, no link.
- Day 7: Write down what people asked. Fix one thing.

## Week 2: show the result
- Day 8-10: Take one small game and run 5 prompts on it. Record before/after clips.
- Day 11: Post the clip on your own social account.
- Day 12: Post once on the itch.io Release announcements board (that is where itch.io says self-promotion belongs). Do not post it anywhere else on itch.io.
- Day 13: Message 3 devs you helped before. Offer the $19 review.
- Day 14: Write what worked.

## Week 3: client #1 and proof
- Do the first review (see 02). Ask for a quote.
- Post the roadmap shape (not their game) as an example.

## Week 4: repeat
- Post a "3 mistakes AI makes in game code" short, using your real experience (4 days build, 4 days debug).
- Offer reviews to 5 more devs. Keep a list of replies.

## Example posts (copy, change the words)
1. "I spent 4 days building my game with AI and 4 more fixing it. So I made a free tool that gives you the prompts in the right order. It runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Link in comments."
2. "Test it yourself: open the page, turn on airplane mode, drop your game zip. It still works."
3. "Before: 1 giant prompt. After: 26 small prompts, one at a time. Here is how the order saves a game."

## What I cannot promise
I cannot promise sales or followers. Track 3 numbers: visits, zips dropped, replies.
