# Fable 5.2 screenshot review (2026-11-14, shots: live15)

SCORE: 6.5
PROFESSIONAL: 6  BEGINNER: 7  ALIVE: 7.5  MONEY-READY: 7

TOP FIVE DEFECTS:
1. desktop-home: the gold target ring is still in the wrong place. It sits as an empty box across the headline ("Stuck on how"), nowhere near the Choose button. The hand and "Start here" label are cut off at the bottom edge, far from the button. Fix: compute the ring and hand position from the Choose button's bounding rect after layout and fonts settle (and on resize); if the position can't be confirmed, show no ring. Hand and label must sit fully inside the viewport, directly beside the button.
2. phone-home: same bug, worse. The ring boxes the subtitle paragraph, and the hand plus "Start here" label sit on top of the "Try the example game" button, covering its text. There is also still a large dead gap between "Your zip never leaves your device" and the buttons. Fix: anchor ring to the Choose button, put the hand above the button bar pointing down so it never overlaps a label, and close the gap by vertically centring the content block or enlarging the gem and text to fill the screen.
3. desktop-kind: the two cards still don't match. PC is smaller and sits lower than Mobile, with different text size, and the hand points at the gap between the cards and Back rather than at a card. Fix: give both cards identical width, height, font size and top alignment (remove any scale/hover state on load), and place the hand clearly under the cards with clear spacing from Back/Next.
4. desktop-plan: the sample card is inconsistent. Task 1 has a prompt box, Task 2 has none, and the bottom of the card is empty. The step row "Pay / Send your game link by email / The plan comes by email" is cramped, with uneven columns and three-line wraps. The left card is dense with small print jammed against the bottom border. Fix: give Task 2 a prompt box like Task 1 (or shrink the card to its content), make the three steps equal-width columns with short one-line labels, and add padding above the PayPal fine print.
5. desktop-assets and desktop-rating: content is left-heavy and the gem floats disconnected at the right edge. On assets the gem crowds the Coin Hop card, and the "Coin Hop asset list" link is orphaned between the list and the buttons. On rating the right third is empty apart from the gem, and the Pip bubble just repeats the score. Fix: on assets, hide or shrink the gem and move the asset-list link into the Coin Hop card; on rating, widen the layout or centre the block, and make Pip say something useful (the next action) instead of repeating the score.

NEXT PROMPT FOR THE BUILDER: The hand-pointer ring is the top bug: on both desktop and phone home it draws an empty gold box over the headline or subtitle instead of around the Choose button, and on phone the hand and "Start here" label cover the "Try the example game" text. Rewrite the pointer so ring and hand are positioned from the target button's live bounding rect after fonts and layout finish, re-measured on resize and scroll, never overlapping any button label, fully inside the viewport, and hidden entirely if the target cannot be measured. Then make the PC and Mobile cards on the kind screen pixel-identical in size, alignment and type; close the dead gap on phone home; give Task 2 in the plan sample a prompt box and tidy the three-step row into equal one-line columns; and move the Coin Hop asset list link into the Coin Hop card while keeping the gem from crowding it. Do not add any new text, colours or sections.
