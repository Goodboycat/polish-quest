# Fable 5.2 screenshot review (2026-11-13, shots: rescore4)

SCORE: 7
PROFESSIONAL: 7  BEGINNER: 7  ALIVE: 6  MONEY-READY: 5

TOP FIVE DEFECTS:
1. desktop-15-plan / phone-15-plan: the offer hides the facts a buyer needs before paying $25. It does not say who writes the plan, how long delivery takes, how many tasks it roughly contains, or what happens if the buyer is unhappy. "Email me your game" comes after "Pay" with no address or instruction shown. Fix: add one line under the title saying who writes it and the delivery time (only if true), and reword step 2 to "After paying you get an email address to send your zip or link". Replace the defensive footer "Nobody can promise how your game will do" with one plain line: "You run the prompts yourself. No results guaranteed."
2. desktop-15-plan: the screen does not fit in one view. The Back button is cut off at the bottom, and the 3-step row wraps awkwardly (1 and 2 on one line, 3 alone on the next). The heading "Stuck on what to improve?" repeats the home question instead of naming the product. Fix: change the heading to "A plan written for your game", drop the duplicate card title, put the 3 steps in one even row as on the phone, and shrink the card padding so Back is fully visible at 720px height.
3. desktop-06-next-2 / phone-06-next-2: the bottom row has three competing actions ("Start the steps", "Want a plan written for your game?", "More"), and "More" is meaningless. On the phone "More" is yellow while the plan link is white, which is inconsistent. The eyebrow "CODE CHECKLIST RATING" is jargon. The grey note "This is the made-up example game. Scored from code only." collides with the "Free, with ads or purchases / Change" block on desktop. Fix: rename the eyebrow "YOUR SCORE", give "More" a real label or remove it, make both links the same style, and move the made-up note to a clear line of its own under the score ring.
4. desktop-01-home, desktop-11-overview, desktop-15-plan, desktop-16-sample: the same dark wireframe polyhedron fills the right half of every screen and does nothing. Only the score screen has a gold, filled version. This is the remaining "looked dead" problem, with half of each screen being decoration. On phone-01-home it sits dim behind the nav and looks like a rendering bug. Fix: make the shape show something per screen (on home, a preview of the score ring and three bars; on the plan, the sample task card), or shrink it. On the phone, hide it or place it clearly below the nav.
5. desktop-11-overview: the map screen is confusing for beginners. The "Ground rules (paste first)" pill is unexplained. Two chapters are highlighted at once (Foundation with a yellow border, Depth and sound with a white border). "Fix Kit" in the nav is never explained anywhere. Small grey text is still hard to read here and elsewhere, including the plan footer and the sample "Done when" line. Fix: use one selected state only, relabel the pill "Step 0: paste these rules first", rename or explain Fix Kit, and raise all small grey text to at least 15px in a lighter grey.

NEXT PROMPT FOR THE BUILDER: Rebuild the #plan screen so a stranger could pay without asking a question, and so it fits in one 1280x720 view with nothing cut off. Make the heading "A plan written for your game" and remove the duplicate card title. Add one truthful line saying who writes the plan and how long delivery takes (ask the owner; do not invent it). Lay out the three steps in a single even row, with step 2 reading "After paying, you get an email address to send your zip or link". Shorten the footer to "Payment by PayPal. You run the prompts yourself. No results guaranteed." in readable light grey at 15px or larger. Replace the empty wireframe shape on the right with a preview of the sample task card (task title, bright prompt box, done-when) that links to #sample. Keep Back fully visible on desktop and phone.
