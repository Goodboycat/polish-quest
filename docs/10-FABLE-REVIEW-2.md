# Fable 5.2 screenshot review (2026-11-12, shots: v7)

SCORE: 6
PROFESSIONAL: 6  BEGINNER: 5  ALIVE: 6  MONEY-READY: 5

TOP FIVE DEFECTS:
1. Rating screen (desktop-06, phone-06): the bars contradict the headline. "Runs smoothly 1.3" is listed as the biggest gap but has the shortest bar, and "First minutes 5.0" has the longest. The caption "(goal weight x distance from 10)" is math jargon a beginner will not parse, and it is unclear whether the numbers are scores or gap sizes. Fix: show each area as a plain score out of 10, sorted worst first, with a one-word label (Weak / OK / Good). Delete the formula line and replace it with "Fix these three first."
2. Rating screen, phone-06: this is a wall of small grey text. There are three "Not found: ... and 2 more. Step 4 (Performance first) fixes this." paragraphs, plus the disclaimer and the formula. The 3D shape sits behind the headline and score ring, hurting legibility. Pip overlaps the "See all 7 areas" row. Fix: one line per area (name, bar, score), with detail on tap. Remove or strongly dim the canvas shape behind text on phone. Move Pip clear of the content.
3. Goal screen (desktop-04, phone-04): the gold outline around the whole four-card group looks like a selected state or focus-ring bug, and it competes with the real selected-card outline in desktop-05. The selected state itself is only a thin gold border, which is too weak. On phone, the pointing hand and "Tap one that fits" tag sit over the bottom edge of the cards and crowd Pip. Fix: drop the group outline. Have the hand point at one card. Give the selected card a filled gold tint and a check mark. Keep the hand in the gap above the buttons.
4. Overview (desktop-11, phone-11): 26 numbered squares with no names look like a dead spreadsheet. The legend "Gold fill = next for you. Outlined = done." makes the user decode it. On desktop, step 23 has a white outline (hover) that clashes with the "outlined = done" rule, and the caption reads "23. Retention" while 2 is the next step. Phone leaves a large empty lower third. Fix: group the steps into 4-5 named chapters with a title on each, and show the next step as a large card with its name and a "Continue" button. Use a distinct hover style and drop the legend.
5. Home (desktop-01) and all screens: the hero art is a dark, near-invisible wireframe icosahedron that says nothing about games or polish. On later screens it is a muddy ghost behind the content. The "Drop your zip here" hand overlaps below the button and repeats the button label. "26 copy-paste prompts" sounds like a chore before any value is shown. Fix: replace the shape with a lit, recognizable 3D object (a game cartridge or controller being polished to a gold shine) that reacts to progress. Change the copy to lead with the outcome: "See what your game is missing, then fix it step by step." Hide the hand after the first interaction.

WHAT WOULD MOVE IT TO 9:
- Make the rating screen a single clear verdict: a big score, one sentence explaining why, three worst areas as tappable one-line rows with consistent bars, and one primary button. Everything else goes behind a tap.
- Give the 3D a job: a bright hero object tied to progress that shines up as steps complete, never placed behind text, with Pip reacting to the score. Right now the art is dim decoration.
- Turn the 26-step grid into a named chapter journey with a visible "next step" card and a progress payoff, so a beginner always knows the one thing to do now and how far there is to go.
