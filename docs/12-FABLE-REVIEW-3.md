# Fable 5.2 screenshot review (2026-11-13, shots: rescore1)

SCORE: 6
PROFESSIONAL: 6  BEGINNER: 6  ALIVE: 6  MONEY-READY: 4

TOP FIVE DEFECTS:
1. Rating screen (desktop-06 and phone-06): the 3D polyhedron sits directly behind the bars, the numbers and the "Moon Cat Runner" headline, so the "1.3 Weak" and "OK" labels are read through gold wireframe. On phone it collides with the title. Fix: on this screen, move the object fully off the text column (right gutter on desktop, hidden on phone) or drop it to a faint static backdrop. Also recolor status labels: "Weak" and "OK" are the same gold, so nothing looks wrong. Make "Weak" a single warning tint with a short bar marker, and "OK" neutral gray.
2. Phone rating and phone overview (phone-06, phone-11): the Pip mascot overlaps "($25)" on the "Ask for a human review" line, covering the price on the only paid offer. Fix: reserve bottom padding equal to the mascot height, or dock Pip into the header on phone; never let it float over content.
3. Overview (desktop-11, phone-11): 26 anonymous number tiles is a spreadsheet, not a map. A beginner cannot tell what step 14 is without tapping. Step 13 is outlined, which the legend says means "done", while the counter reads 0/6 (it is probably a hover or focus state, but it contradicts the legend). The desktop footer says "13. Earned abilities" while the phone says "Next: 1. Audit and memory file". Fix: show one chapter at a time with step names; give focus a style distinct from "done"; make the footer line consistently show the next step.
4. Rating screen (desktop-06): too many competing actions on one screen: "Start the steps", "Ask the AI for a second opinion", "Copy the report", "Change", "See all 7 areas", "Ask for a human review", plus tappable rows. The secondary button is wider than the primary. Fix: keep one primary ("Start the steps") and one quiet text link row; move "second opinion" and "Copy the report" behind a single "More" link; give the $25 human review its own small card rather than a gray "Still stuck?" sentence.
5. Goal screen on phone (phone-04): the gold outline wraps all four cards at once, which reads as "all selected" or a focus bug, and the pointing hand overlaps the bottom edge of the cards while Pip floats over the empty row. The disabled "Continue" is muddy brown and looks broken rather than waiting. Fix: remove the group outline; pulse one card edge at a time or just keep the hand; style disabled Continue as a gray outline with the label "Pick one first".

WHAT WOULD MOVE IT TO 9:
- Give the 3D art a job per screen instead of the same dark polyhedron everywhere: on the rating screen, make the object visibly react to the score (fills or lights up by area), and keep it out of the text column at every size. At present the hero object is nearly invisible black on black and adds little life.
- Make the rating trustworthy and actionable at a glance: distinct states for weak, OK and strong, a one-line plain-language reason under the weakest area without tapping, and a clear "code checklist, nobody played it" note beside the dial rather than in small gray text under the goal.
- Turn the paid offer and the step map into finished product surfaces: a proper human review card (what you get, turnaround, price) that the mascot never covers, and a chapter-by-chapter step view with names and progress, consistent between desktop and phone.
