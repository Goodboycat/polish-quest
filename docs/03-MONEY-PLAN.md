# 3. Money plan
Rule: sale money first pays for ONE thing. Pick the better web address first, not the Genspark subscription.
Why: the site works with no AI at all. A domain makes people trust the link and does not run out. The subscription ($24.99/month per the research notes) only speeds up YOUR custom reviews, and you can still do client #1 by hand. Buy the subscription when you have 3 or more paying clients waiting.
A `.com` at Cloudflare Registrar showed US$10.46 a year for registration and renewal (cfdomainpricing.com, 2026-11-11; Cloudflare says it sells at cost). Confirm in your dashboard before paying. Other registrars can charge more at renewal.
Genspark prices, verified on genspark.ai/helpcenter/membership-plans: Free = 100 credits a day up to a lifetime limit. Plus = $24.99 a month, or $19.99 a month billed yearly ($239.99 a year), 10,000 credits a month. Refund window for most countries is 72 hours (EU, UK, Turkey 14 days), and only if you used under 50%. You get ONE refund per customer. Upgrading replaces the daily free credits, you do not get both.
Genspark API: not usable on the Free plan (checked with a live call).

| 1 | $19 | about $17 | Domain (about $10), keep the rest | A real address, a more trusted link |
