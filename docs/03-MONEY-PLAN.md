# 3. Money plan
Rule: sale money first pays for ONE thing. Pick the better web address first, not the Genspark subscription.
Why: the site works with no AI at all. A domain makes people trust the link and does not run out. The subscription ($24.99/month per the research notes) only speeds up YOUR custom reviews, and you can still do client #1 by hand. Buy the subscription when you have 3 or more paying clients waiting.
A domain costs about $10 a year on most registrars. Check the price on the registrar page. Renewals can cost more than year one.

| Sale # | Money in (before fees) | After fees (about 8-10%) | Spend on | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $19 | about $17 | Domain (about $10), keep the rest | A real address, a more trusted link |
| 2 | $19 | about $17 | Save | Cushion for refunds and fees |
| 3 | $19 | about $17 | Save, total near $40 | Enough for one month of the subscription |
| 4-5 | $19 each | about $17 each | Genspark Plus ($24.99 first month) | Faster reviews, more clients |
| 6+ | raise to $29 | about $26 each | Keep 50% for costs, 50% for growth | Longer-term plan, bigger bundle |
Fees: your payment method sets the real number. Replace the "about" values with real ones after sale 1.
