# Fable 5.2 screenshot review (2026-11-13, shots: rescore3)

SCORE: 6
PROFESSIONAL: 7  BEGINNER: 6  ALIVE: 6  MONEY-READY: 4

TOP FIVE DEFECTS:
1. desktop-01-home / desktop-15-plan: the free product and the paid product are described in the same words. Home promises "Get a plan made for it: one prompt per task, in order" for free, and #plan sells "A plan made for your game" for $25. A buyer cannot tell what the $25 adds. Fix: on #plan add one plain line under the title that states the difference (e.g. "The free steps are the same for every game. This plan is written for yours."), and reword the home sub-line so it describes the free checklist and steps, not "a plan made for it".
2. desktop-06-next-2 / phone-06-next-2: the score screen is the moment the user feels the pain (5.0, "Weak"), yet it has no path to the offer. The only route to the offer is a small grey nav word, "Plan". Fix: add one quiet secondary link beside "Start the steps", such as "Want this done for your game? See the full plan", going to #plan. Remove the vague "More" link or name it.
3. desktop-16-sample: the sample overflows the screen, and the closing text is cut off at the bottom with no visible button. That breaks the one-screen rule and leaves the sample as a dead end. The prompt, the actual thing being sold, is the smallest and greyest text on the screen. Fix: show one task per screen with Next/Back, or shrink to fit. Put the prompt in a distinct bright copy-style box, and pin an "Order for $25" button and a "Back to plan" button so they are always visible.
4. desktop-15-plan / phone-15-plan: the order details are too thin to trust. "It is sent by email after you pay" does not say when it arrives, how the seller gets the game, or who writes it. That also clashes with the home claim "Your zip never leaves your device". There are three buttons of nearly equal weight, and the disclaimer "cannot promise a good game or any sales" is the last thing read before paying. Fix: add a 3-step "How it works" row (pay, send your zip or link, plan arrives by email within a stated time). Demote "Ask a question first" to a text link. Reword the disclaimer to something honest but neutral and move it below the card.
5. phone-01-home: the top third is a nearly invisible, cropped grey polyhedron that sits partly behind the nav, with an empty gap above the buttons. It looks dead and unfinished. The pointing hand overlaps the main button. On desktop, the same dull wireframe shape fills half of every screen (overview, plan, sample) and shows nothing. Fix: on phone, shrink the shape, tint it gold and place it fully below the nav, or remove it. Move the hand so it sits clear of the button. On #plan and #sample, replace the shape with a picture of the actual plan document.

NEXT PROMPT FOR THE BUILDER: Make the $25 offer understandable and reachable. On #plan, add one plain sentence saying how the paid plan differs from the free steps, plus a 3-step "how it works" row (pay, send your game, plan arrives by email, with a real delivery time supplied by the owner). Keep one yellow button, turn "Ask a question first" into a text link, and move the softened disclaimer below the card. On the score screen, add a secondary link "See the full plan for your game" beside "Start the steps". Reword the home sub-line so it describes the free checklist rather than the paid plan. Make #sample fit one screen, with the prompt in a bright copy-style box and an always-visible "Order for $25" and "Back" button.
