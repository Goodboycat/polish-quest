Game: Tiny Boatball! (Roguelike or dungeon run, LOVE (Lua))
Rules this serves: 10 Retention

GOAL
Find the moments where players quit Tiny Boatball! and give them a real reason to continue right there. Use evidence, not a guess. No tracking, no tricks, no guilt.

LOOK AT FIRST (read only, change nothing yet)

- The short goals from Prompt 14 and the main milestones from Prompt 5. Retention reasons should plug into these, not into a new system.
- What the player sees right after a loss, after finishing a goal, and right after loading the game.

BUILD (small steps, in this order)

2. ASK ME to pick the top 3 quit risks to fix. Wait for my answer. Do not fix the rest.
3. For each chosen moment, add ONE reason to continue that fits the game: a visible next step (a goal that is already half done), a small unfinished thing the player can see, a near reward, or a "one more try" that starts in one tap after a loss.
4. After a loss or the end of a session, show what the player gained (a tiny bar or icon, no text wall) and the single next thing to try, with a one-tap button to go again. The loss must teach something (Prompt 11).
5. Make coming back easy: when the game loads, put the player back where they were, with the next goal showing. Never make them dig through menus.
6. [CHECK FIRST - something like this may already exist] Save whatever the new reasons need (a bump of the save version and an upgrade step for old saves).
