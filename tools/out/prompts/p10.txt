GOAL
Make every hit in Tiny Boatball! feel heavy: knockback, a very short hit-pause, and stretchy attacks. Enemies must be a real threat, not a punching bag.

LOOK AT FIRST (read only, change nothing yet)
- How attacks, hits and damage work today: where hits are detected, what happens to the target and to the attacker.
- How enemies move, attack and react. Which ones are easy to ignore.
- What the player sees and hears on a hit. Count the layers (movement, flash, sound, shake).
- How long a hit lasts, and whether the player can tell what hit them.

BUILD (small steps, in this order)
1. Knockback: a hit pushes the target away a short distance, and heavier hits push more. Use a short eased movement, not an instant jump.
2. [ALREADY IN THE GAME - check it works, do not rebuild] Hit-pause: freeze the action for a very short moment (about 2 to 5 frames) on a solid hit. Make it a setting so it can be tuned or turned off. Never freeze the UI or the log.
3. Stretchy attacks: a quick wind-up, a fast stretch on the hit frame, then a settle. Use scale and position only.
4. [ALREADY IN THE GAME - check it works, do not rebuild] A small camera shake only on heavy hits, with a strength setting and a reduce-motion off switch. Never shake on every tap.
5. Make enemies a real threat: each enemy type gets one clear tell (a wind-up the player can read) and one real consequence. Test that a player who ignores them loses health.
6. Give hits clear feedback in this order: the target reacts, a short flash, one sound, one number or icon if needed. Nothing more.
7. Tune with settings, not magic numbers: put knockback, hit-pause and shake strength in one settings object and write the values in the memory file.

DO NOT TOUCH
- Do not change damage numbers, health or difficulty beyond what a real threat needs. Tell me before you change any.
- Do not make effects that last long or cover the screen.
- Do not add new enemy types. Make the existing ones a real threat.
