Game: Tiny Boatball! (Roguelike or dungeon run, LOVE (Lua))
Rules this serves: 9 Economy

GOAL
Money buys decisions, not more of the same. Even a rich player still has strategy.

LOOK AT FIRST (read only, change nothing yet)
- [CHECK FIRST - something like this may already exist] Every way the player earns and spends money, points or resources. Write them as a list of sources and sinks.
- What the player buys today. Is it "more of the same" (bigger number) or a real choice?
- What a very rich player does. Can they buy everything and stop thinking?

BUILD (small steps, in this order)
1. Write the economy map in the memory file: sources, sinks, and what each purchase changes.
2. Find purchases that are only "more of the same" (for example +10% damage again and again). Propose replacing each with a choice (two options where taking one makes the other worse or impossible, or a trade-off).
3. ASK ME which replacements I approve. Wait for my answer.
4. Add at least one sink that stays interesting when rich: a choice with a cost that cannot be bought away (limited slots, a trade-off, a risk, a long-term bet).
5. Make prices follow the player's progress, so a rich player faces harder choices, not an empty shop.
6. Show prices with an icon and a number. Keep shop text to a minimum.
7. Do not change the amount of money in the game without telling me first.

DO NOT TOUCH
- Do not add real-money purchases, ads or any outside payment.
- Do not remove existing items. Replace or reshape them only if I approve.
- Do not make the game grindy.

- These files and folders must never be edited (read them if you must, never write):
    toolkit/lib/

- A very rich player still has a meaningful choice to make.
